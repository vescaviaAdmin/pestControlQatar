FAQ’S

Frequently Asked Questions

What pest control services do you provide?

We handle common residential and commercial pest issues, including cockroaches, ants, bed bugs, termites, rodents, mosquitoes, flies, and general preventive treatments.

Are your treatments safe for families and pets?

Yes. We use controlled application methods and guide you on any simple precautions before and after treatment, especially for homes with children, pets, or sensitive areas.

How quickly can you visit my location?

Availability depends on your area and service type, but we aim to schedule inspections and treatments as quickly as possible across Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Khor, Al Rayyan, and nearby areas.

Do you offer one-time and regular pest control plans?

Yes. You can book a single treatment or choose a recurring maintenance plan for ongoing protection in homes, offices, restaurants, warehouses, and other properties.
